About.

I’m Daniel Medin. I make sound and software.

I’m a sound designer and project manager working across film, games, campaigns and digital products. I founded Ljudbyrån in 2000 and am an owner of MRC Collective.

Sound

At Ljudbyrån, I’ve spent more than two decades making sound for commercial and corporate productions. My work includes sound design for films, games and digital campaigns, including projects in the automotive industry. The studio also works with voice recordings, music, mixing and mastering.

I work as a sound designer, engineer, producer and creative director. Thousands of productions have passed through the studio, each with its own brief, audience and way of being heard. I like the mix of creative decisions and practical delivery that comes with getting sound from an idea to a finished piece.

Software

MRC Collective began as a group of professional developers building projects together in their spare time. Those projects grew into workshops, coding events and client work. We now build booking systems, reporting tools, games and other digital services.

Much of our work supports youth centres and the organisations around them. aVenue and Lokalbokning help manage rooms and activities; Malmö Fritid supports day-to-day work and reporting; and The Logbook helps youth organisations document and follow up their work. We build with the people who use these systems, so the tools fit what happens in their working day.

Alongside development, I manage web and software projects. I’m used to moving between clients, creative teams and developers, making sure decisions are clear and the work keeps moving.
